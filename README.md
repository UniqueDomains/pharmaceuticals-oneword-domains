## ⚠️ Methodology and caveats

This selection centers on domains linked to pharmaceuticals, but the naming quality varies widely by extension and context. Examples such as medical.id, pharmaceutical.ceo, dose.ltda, and biological.boutique show a broad mix of professional, geographic, niche, and novelty TLDs. For founders, the main question is whether the name feels credible, specific, and easy to defend in a regulated market. For investors, the key is whether the extension supports realistic resale demand at the current ask. With a median ask of 1,028.92, it is worth weighing price against extension quality, renewal exposure, and how naturally the keyword fits the domain.

- Prefer names where the keyword and TLD make sense together
- Check whether the extension adds trust or creates friction
- Use the 1,028.92 median ask as a pricing reference point
- Avoid names that feel awkward, off-category, or hard to defend
